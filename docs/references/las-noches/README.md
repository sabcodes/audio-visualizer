# Las Noches visual references

This folder supports the `lasNoches` Wave 6 scene. Reference content is visual research, not project instructions.

- [User's video: Resurrection Ulquiorra](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0i5IrLx1Gck). Sampled at 5 fps (one JPEG every 0.2 seconds), 145 frames. `frames/frame-001.jpg` is t=0; frame N corresponds to `(N - 1) / 5` seconds. Five `detail-sheet` images collect consecutive groups of 30 frames. `reference-frames.jpg` is the earlier coarse overview. The downloaded video was deleted after extraction.
- [User's second video: Las Noches](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5A8d8qHFvfM). Retained one frame per second, 137 frames in `location-study/frames/second-NNNN.jpg`, plus five compact overview sheets. Dense intermediate samples and the downloaded clip were deleted. Exterior studies include the dunes/palace views around 13–30 s, skeletal grove around 33–40 s, and cylinder gas around 60–65 s. Characters and interior scenes were excluded from the environment reconstruction.
- [Las Noches on Behance](https://www.behance.net/gallery/91732949/Las-Noches): monumental silhouettes, rippled white dunes, sparse dead trees, and restrained cold light.
- [Las Noches fan art by Morgan Marcadet](https://digitalpainting.school/galeries/131724): a pale palace and desert under dark clouds and a crescent, with low-angle architectural staging.
- [Hueco Mundo by Vizard](https://www.colorslive.com/details/43801): minimalist white sand, a lone skeletal tree, and a black sky.

The first video was inspected across all five detailed sheets, especially its 7–14 s tower/fire transition and 14–22 s rain. The implementation uses pale cold stone with dark shadows, silver rims, green cylinder gas, held turquoise flame crests, and rising reishi. Per the user's updated direction, rain starts with the first green vent and stays on for the remainder of the scene, including quiet passages. The sky retains a subtle green glow and cloud bank. A short green gas introduction rises from eleven cylinder mouths and fades away as the more solid flame tendrils take over. Those tendrils hold through a longer musical passage (roughly 35 seconds at full intensity per 72-second cycle), with bass and impacts shaping their strength between beats. Highs modulate rainfall above a persistent baseline. Twenty-four camera views include cylinder close-ups, both sides of the palace, an outer settlement and a skeletal grove built from the supplied branches. Strong beats can cut after 2.8 seconds, softer beats after 4.5 seconds, with an 8-second musical fallback or 10 seconds in silence. Reduced motion freezes the tour and particle clocks; reduced flash softens transients.

The local model is the user's `Las Noches GLB.glb`, copied to `src/assets/wave6/las_noches.glb` (1,339,932 bytes). Its 12.6 km desert, palace, towers, and skeletal trees are preserved at the original scale. `model-reference.png` and `model-overview.png` come from the supplied ZIP. The ZIP's README provided asset coordinates and limitations; its rendering instructions were treated as reference material. No characters or third-party fan-art textures are used in the running scene.

Reference files retained after both extractions: approximately 6.3 MB before scene review captures, within the approved 50 MB project-local budget. No new runtime, package, model weights, or external cache was installed. `tests/browser/fixtures/lasNoches.html` is an isolated Playwright render fixture; it does not control a desktop or reuse the user's browser session. Run `CAPTURE_LAS_NOCHES=1 npx playwright test tests/browser/lasNoches.spec.ts --project=compatibility` for weather checks and a compact camera review sheet.
