# Water 7 / City of Water

Select `<experimental-wave6> Water 7 / City of Water` in the visualizer, or use `modern.html?mode=water7`.

The supplied model stays at `Model assets/Wave6 assets /Water 7 v2 model.glb`; Vite bundles that file directly. The source model and its package are unchanged.

24 directed shots visit the whole island, eight waterways, harbor, gateways, aqueducts, fountain and city streets. The idle reel lasts about 2 minutes 38 seconds; strong musical onsets can finish a shot near its end. Later shots visit afternoon and blue hour before returning to daylight.

Bass raises ocean swells, curling breakers and boat motion. Mids lift the fountain jets and accelerate canal currents; highs add small surface glints. Onsets send a surge through the breaking waves and fountain. Foam washes against the seawall and spray follows each impact. The supplied waterfall sheets carry downward-moving streaks. Reduced motion freezes the animation, and reduced flashes cap beat accents.

`camera-review.jpg` contains 23 rendered views; `portrait.jpg` checks a vertical island framing. These are captured by the project's headless WebGL2 test fixture, not model-package previews.

Validation: production build, full unit suite (248 tests), and two Water 7 browser checks. Browser coverage includes all 24 shots at three points in each camera path, model loading, moving water pixels, beat response, reduced motion, reduced flashes, portrait framing, switching modes, and disposal.
